# Skip de-anonymizers, capture visitors through WhatsApp

None of the AI visitor-identification tools will reliably put names or emails to anonymous Indian visitors, and the person-level ones are not lawful for EU visitors. Every person-level tool whose coverage I could verify identifies individuals in the US only. That covers RB2B, Vector, Leadpipe, Retention.com, Apollo and Instantly. Warmly and Customers.ai say their coverage is wider, but neither publishes a match rate outside the US. These tools don't extract an email from the visit. They match a cookie or device ID against a third-party identity graph that is dense only where US adtech has collected consented emails. Company-level reverse-IP tools (Leadfeeder/Dealfront, Snitcher, Albacross, Factors.ai, HubSpot Breeze) work globally in principle. In practice, about 96% of Indian internet subscriptions are wireless, and Jio and Airtel traffic sits behind carrier-grade NAT and IPv6. An SMB owner browsing on a phone therefore resolves to "Reliance Jio", and the tool throws the visit away. On the law: under GDPR and ePrivacy, person-level de-anonymization of EU visitors needs prior consent, which it never asks for. India's DPDP Act puts its consent and notice duties into force around **13 May 2027**. After that date, covert identity matching has no lawful basis in India either. Leads found this way also can't be messaged on WhatsApp, because Meta requires the recipient's own opt-in. For converszio.com, the tools that work are first-party ones: a WhatsApp click-to-chat entry point (which doubles as a product demo), free tools with a one-field WhatsApp opt-in, Meta Pixel plus the server-side Conversions API (CAPI) behind a consent banner, Click-to-WhatsApp ads, and PostHog stitching anonymous sessions to accounts at signup.

## Every person-level tool is US-only in practice

The person-level category looks crowded, but the vendors' own pages narrow it quickly. **RB2B's pricing page labels person-level identification "US Only"**, and its FAQ says contact-level ID is US only while company-level ID is global ([RB2B pricing](https://www.rb2b.com/pricing)). The current plans are a free plan with 150 company-level resolutions a month, Starter at **$79/mo** (LinkedIn URLs, no emails), Pro from **$149/mo** (business emails, CRM integrations) and Pro+ from $199/mo. RB2B's own match-rate figures disagree with each other: the pricing page says Pro reaches 15–20% contact-level coverage, while a help article says Pro users "typically see about 40–45%" on US traffic ([RB2B support](https://support.rb2b.com/en/articles/11155068-person-level-identification)). Retention.com's help centre says only US visitors are identified, with "no option to include other countries" ([Retention help](https://intercom.help/retention-self-serve/en/articles/14327401-which-visitors-are-identified)). Vector is reportedly "geofenced to the U.S." ([Vector FAQ snippet](https://learn.vector.co/articles/5062232408-faq-help-center)) and starts around **$399/mo** ([Docket](https://docket.io/resources/research/vector-pricing)). Leadpipe calls itself "US-first", falls back to company level for EU/UK visitors, and starts at $147/mo for 500 credits ([Leadpipe](https://www.leadpipe.com/blog/how-we-built-the-leadpipe-identity-graph/); [Leadpipe pricing](https://leadpipe.com/blog/visitor-identification-pricing)). Apollo matches contacts "currently available for US-based visitors" ([Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/product/website-visitor-identification)). Primer keeps visitors outside the US anonymized ([Primer](https://www.sayprimer.com/blog/unlimited-website-reveal)).

Warmly is the main exception on paper. Its blog says person-level ID "isn't US-bound" ([Warmly blog](https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/leadpipe-alternatives)), but independent comparisons describe it as mainly US because of GDPR and US-centric identity graphs ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/rb2b-vs-warmly)). Its de-anonymization product also costs **$10,000/yr and up**, which is out of scale for an SMB SaaS ([Warmly pricing](https://www.warmly.ai/p/pricing)). Customers.ai, Opensend and Retention.com are B2C ecommerce tools that feed personal emails into abandoned-cart flows. Customers.ai's only evidence of non-US use is an informal Product Hunt comment ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/customers-ai-website-identity-pixel?page=2)). Opensend lists the United States as its only available country ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10015708/Opensend/pricing/)).

The way these tools work explains the geography. RB2B says it cross-references "first- and third-party cookies, device identifiers, and IP address analysis" across a large network, then enriches the result from LinkedIn and career databases ([RB2B support](https://support.rb2b.com/en/articles/11155068-person-level-identification)). Leadpipe describes a graph built from hashed signup emails supplied by partner publishers, app device IDs and login events, linked through a pixel on about 5 million sites ([Leadpipe](https://www.leadpipe.com/blog/how-we-built-the-leadpipe-identity-graph/)). A visitor can be matched only if their browser has already passed through that partner network. An Indian kirana distributor on an Android phone almost never has. Accuracy is weak even in the US. A competitor-commissioned audit put RB2B's correct-identification rate at **52%** ([Leadpipe audit](https://leadpipe.com/blog/visitor-identification-accuracy-independent-test-results)). In a practitioner test reported second-hand, two tools each identified the tester's company and neither identified the tester ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/rb2b-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons)). No vendor publishes a person-level match rate for India, and I found no independent India test. The working assumption should be close to zero.

## Jio's shared mobile IPs defeat company-level tools too

Reverse-IP tools work only when a visit comes from an IP block registered to the company, such as an office network or a corporate VPN. On mobile, "the IP tells you the carrier (T-Mobile, Vodafone, Reliance Jio) and not much more" ([Abmatic](https://abmatic.ai/blog/reverse-ip-lookup-dying-agent-era-2026)). The vendors know this and filter such visits out deliberately. Leadfeeder blocks ISP-resolved visits because "ISP visitors are not interesting" ([Leadfeeder help](https://help.leadfeeder.com/articles/1271815-what-are-isps-and-why-is-leadfeeder-blocking-them)), and Snitcher "automatically filters out ISPs" ([Snitcher](https://snitcher.com/product/visitor-id)). India's traffic mix is close to the worst case. TRAI data for December 2025 counts **983 million wireless subscribers out of 1,029 million**, about 95.6% ([Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/internet-subscribers-in-india-grew-1-1-in-october-december-2025-quarter-trai/)). Reliance Jio is about **96.5% IPv6-capable**, with IPv4 traffic sent through carrier-grade NAT ([RIPE list](https://mailman.ripe.net/archives/list/members-discuss@ripe.net/message/OGKHZF73OP6DTWOB7YEMBNH6V7GSW2YC/)). That means dozens to hundreds of subscribers share each public IPv4 address ([Join Massive](https://www.joinmassive.com/glossary/what-is-cgnat)). The only India figure I found, Happierleads' "15–35%" for India and Southeast Asia, comes from a reverse-IP vendor and doesn't say whether it is company- or person-level ([Happierleads](https://happierleads.com/blog/2026-b2b-website-visitor-identification-benchmark-report)). Vendors put mobile-heavy sites at about 15% company match ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/reverse-ip-tracking)). Converszio should treat that as a ceiling, and most of those matches would be mid-size firms rather than its SMB customers.

Factors.ai, founded in Bangalore, is the one tool that claims to be built for Indian ISP coverage. The source behind that claim is a Factors-friendly comparison with no stated method. It says Indian SaaS teams identify "3–5x more domestic visitors" than with Leadfeeder, whose domestic match rate it puts below 30% ([moderninbound](https://moderninbound.com/blog/factors-ai-vs-leadfeeder)). A better IP database can map Indian corporate IP ranges more accurately. It can't recover a single phone from behind a shared carrier address. Company-level tools are useful for one visitor segment: agencies, larger businesses and partners browsing from office networks. The cheap way to test them is Leadfeeder's free Lite plan (100 companies a month, 7-day history) or a 14-day Snitcher trial, run on real traffic and split by network type.

## DPDP's May 2027 start makes covert identification unlawful in India

Under EU law, person-level de-anonymization has two hurdles and clears neither. ePrivacy Art. 5(3) requires prior consent for any storing of or access to information on the device. The EDPB's Guidelines 2/2023 extend that explicitly to tracking pixels, unique identifiers and some IP-based tracking ([EDPB Guidelines 2/2023](https://edpb.europa.eu:443/system/files/2024-10/edpb_guidelines_202302_technical_scope_art_53_eprivacydirective_v2_en_0.pdf)). Separately, GDPR requires a lawful basis for the profiling, and covert matching fails the transparency requirement ([Happierleads GDPR 2026](https://happierleads.com/blog/gdpr-website-visitor-identification-eu-rules-2026)). That is why RB2B reportedly geofences EU visitors out of person-level ID ([Leadpipe](https://leadpipe.com/blog/is-rb2b-gdpr-compliant)). Regulators do enforce here. In September 2025 CNIL fined **Google €325M and Shein €150M** over cookies set without valid consent ([PrivacyLaws](https://www.privacylaws.com/news/cnil-fines-google-325-million-and-shein-150-million/)). Company-level reverse-IP is the defensible option in the EU. Leadfeeder itself treats IP-based identification as personal-data processing under legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). It also warns that the customer's own legitimate interest is "not automatically available" and that cookies are an ePrivacy consent question ([Leadfeeder legal FAQ](https://help.leadfeeder.com/en/articles/13921886-leadfeeder-legal-faq); [Leadfeeder blog](https://www.leadfeeder.com/blog/website-visitor-identification/website-visitor-tracking-in-a-post-gdpr-world-what-b2bs-need-to-know/)). Consent-management vendors take the stricter view that the tracker should not load before consent ([ConsentStack](https://www.consentstack.io/vendors/leadfeeder)).

India's position is a deadline rather than a prohibition today. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. Consent Manager registration starts around November 2026. Notice, consent, the other substantive duties and the penalty sections start around **12–13 May 2027** ([AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=87799); [The Privacy Labs](https://theprivacylabs.com/blogs/dpdp-13-november-2026-what-starts)). As of August 2026 the Data Protection Board reportedly had no members appointed, and a proposal to shorten the timeline had not been notified ([dpdprules.org](https://dpdprules.org/blog/dpdp-18-months-to-12-months-proposal)). Once the duties are in force, consent must be "free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous" with a clear affirmative action. Non-essential tracking cookies therefore need opt-in ([Samagra Law via Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/cracking-the-cookie-code-digital-cookies-under-the-dpdp-act)). Section 7's "legitimate uses" cover data the person *voluntarily provided* for a stated purpose, plus State, medical and employment uses ([DPDPA s.7](https://dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-2/section7.html)). An email matched from a US identity graph was never provided by the visitor, so marketing on it has no basis other than consent. The relevant penalty band reaches **INR 50 crore** per breach ([dpdpa.com Schedule](https://dpdpa.com/theschedule.html)). The rule that binds hardest today is Meta's. WhatsApp Business policy allows messaging only people who gave the business their number and opted in to its messages ([WhatsApp Business Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)). TRAI's amended TCCCPR rules separately govern promotional SMS and calls, including a 90-day lockout after an opt-out ([Khaitan & Co](https://khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/Efforts-to-Curb-Spam-Continue-TRAI-Releases-Second-Amendment-to-the-TCCCPR-2018)). A de-anonymized lead is a contact Converszio can't lawfully put into the channel its own product runs on.

## The working playbook is opt-in and runs through WhatsApp

Retargeting needs small audiences now, which helps a launch-stage site. Google cut its minimum remarketing list to **100 active users** across all networks during 2025 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-lowers-audience-size-limits-across-ads-466816)). Meta Custom Audiences reportedly need about 100 matched people ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-custom-audiences)). LinkedIn needs 300 and only starts collecting once its tag is installed ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/matched-audiences/website-visitors-retargeting?view=li-lms-2025-01)). Meta is far cheaper in India: Superads puts the average CPM at about **$1.98 against $19.92 globally** ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india)). LinkedIn clicks cost ₹150–650 ([Upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/linkedin-ads-pricing-by-industry-india-2026/)), which doesn't pay back for a plan priced in the low thousands of rupees a month. Click-to-WhatsApp ads fit the product directly. Vendors estimate ₹12–120 per chat ([Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-india-2026); [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)). A reply within 24 hours opens a **72-hour window in which all messages are free** ([SleekFlow](https://help.sleekflow.io/en_US/understanding-click-to-whatsapp-ads-ctwa-and-the-72-hour-free-window)). Each chat started this way gives Converszio a phone number with opt-in, and it shows the visitor the product working.

Indian competitors have converged on the same playbook. A fetch of their homepage HTML on 2026-11-05 found Google Tag Manager containers on all five. AiSensy and DoubleTick load the Meta Pixel, Gallabox loads a Google Ads tag, and Interakt, WATI and AiSensy use Microsoft Clarity. All five lead with "Start free trial" or Calendly "Book a demo" CTAs. WATI and Gallabox link directly to their own WhatsApp number. WATI, Gallabox and DoubleTick offer free tools such as wa.me link and QR generators, and WATI and AiSensy add cost calculators ([wati.io](https://www.wati.io/); [aisensy.com](https://www.aisensy.com/); [gallabox.com](https://www.gallabox.com/); [doubletick.io](https://www.doubletick.io/)). Lead magnets roughly double popup conversion (7.73% vs 3.83% on mobile) ([Crazy Egg](https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/?p=109699)). B2B SaaS exit-intent popups convert about 2–5% ([Wisepops](https://wisepops.com/use-cases/exit-popups)). For stitching sessions, PostHog gives every visitor an anonymous `distinct_id` and merges that history into the person when `identify(userId)` runs at signup ([PostHog docs](https://posthog.com/docs/data/identify)). The identify call should use the database user ID, not the email, so the client and server agree ([PostHog PR #2744](https://github.com/PostHog/wizard-workbench/pull/2744)). Meta CAPI deduplicates browser and server events that share an `event_id` ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/deduplicate-pixel-and-server-events)). It doesn't remove the consent requirement: Meta's Business Tools Terms make the advertiser warrant notice and "all required consents" ([Meta terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms)).

### Prioritized recommendation for converszio.com

Today the site loads only `@vercel/analytics` and has no pixel, tag manager or consent banner. The CSP in `next.config.mjs` runs in Report-Only mode, so every new origin has to be added there before enforcement is switched on.

Audiences are not retroactive, so start early. | Free tag; ads from a few thousand ₹/month |

Send `distinct_id`, `_fbp`, `_fbc` and `gclid` in the signup body. | Turns every anonymous session into known history at signup and gives a funnel view. | Free tier; 1–2 days |

## Conclusion

The question changes once you look at the identifiers. US vendors sell an email matched from a cookie, but Indian SMB owners' cookies were never collected into those graphs, and their IPs belong to Jio. The identifier that works in India is a WhatsApp number, and a person hands it over only by choosing to start a chat. Converszio already builds the tooling for that. Its best lead-capture system is the product itself on its own landing page, which also makes the case to prospects that WhatsApp-first capture works for SMBs.

Timing makes the consent work more pressing. Building consent gating, CAPI and PostHog stitching before May 2027 is cheap. Retrofitting them later means discarding data collected without consent, and any de-anonymized list built in the meantime would have to go too. Two things in this report are unverified: Factors.ai's India coverage claims and the competitor-sourced accuracy audits. The only evidence that matters for Converszio is a two-week free-tier test on its own traffic. Given the network data, that test will most likely confirm the decision rather than change it.
